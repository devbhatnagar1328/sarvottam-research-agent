# SRVCABLE — research brief



## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables makes power cables, wires and conductors [S1].
- Revenue from operations increased by 18.0% to ₹1,248 crore in Q1 FY27 [S1].
- EBITDA was ₹140 crore (+2.6% YoY), profit after tax was ₹82 crore (+5.9%), and EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2% [S1].
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S1].

## Bull case
- Management says demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [S1].
- The order book increased from ₹3,150 crore to ₹3,900 crore year over year [S1].
- Expansion through the Bharuch plant, expected to add 30% to cable capacity [S1].

## Bear case
- EBITDA rose 2.6% while margin fell 170 bps to 11.2% during a sharp rise in copper prices; two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses with one-to-two-quarter reset lags [S1, S4].
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days, indicating slower payments from large customers [S4].
- The company received a ₹46.3 crore GST demand for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 input tax credits, and plans to appeal [S2].

## Open questions
- The company release reports Q1 revenue of ₹1,248 crore (+18%), while Business Daily reports ₹1,428 crore (+35%); the company filing is the primary source. Why do the figures differ? [S1, S5].
- A March 2024 article reported 35% of promoter holding pledged; the June 2026 NSE filing reports 4.1%. When did the pledge decline, and what changed in between? [S3, S6].
